# SheroTech Workspace Rules

- **Strict Adherence to Documentation Guidelines**: Before making ANY changes to the codebase, including structural, architectural, visual, or functional, you MUST strictly adhere to the guidelines set in the `docs/` folder (such as `DESIGN_GUIDE.md`, `BRAND_STYLE_REFERENCE.md`, `COMPONENT_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md`). Always verify that your implementation aligns with the established UI/UX consistency, OKLCH color system, typography rules, accessibility requirements, and performance best practices.
- **SHERO Brand & Identity Adherence**: All copy, design, and code must reflect the SHERO identity as defined in `docs/Company/`. 
  - **Philosophy**: Technology exists to expand what's possible, remove barriers, and create opportunities. Build with purpose and intention.
  - **Values**: Prioritize Purpose, Integrity, Ownership, and Reliability.
  - **Tone & Voice**: Communication must be Clear, Human, Confident, Thoughtful, and Optimistic. Avoid jargon, corporate speak, arrogance, and hype. Explain ideas simply and focus on people over technology.
  - **Customer Experience**: Interactions should listen first, keep things simple, build trust over time, and leave the customer feeling understood and empowered.
  - **Architecture**: Whether working on SHERO Technology, Health, Finance, Education, or other ventures, they must all align as parts of the master SHERO brand sharing this common philosophy.
