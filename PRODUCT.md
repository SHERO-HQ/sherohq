## Platform

## Users

- **Local Businesses & Organizations in Ghana/West Africa:** Seeking dependable technology partners for bespoke software development, managed IT support, and API/systems integrations without corporate overhead or inflated promises.
- **Social Media Sellers:** Merchants operating on WhatsApp, Instagram, and TikTok looking for specialized commerce management tooling (Merchander waitlist).
- **Pharmacies & Health Outlets:** Community pharmacies needing straightforward inventory and operating systems (Pharmasyst waitlist).
- **Technology Buyers & Individuals:** Everyday users and professionals seeking thoroughly inspected, reliable refurbished hardware and laptops (e-commerce shop included in architecture, currently paused).

## Product Purpose

SHERO is a technology company based in Tamale, Ghana, that builds proprietary software products while providing targeted technology services (custom software, managed IT, system integrations) and tested refurbished hardware for people, businesses, and communities.

Success means:
1. Visitors clearly understand what SHERO does today and what it is building next within seconds.
2. 100% truthful claims across all surfaces—every service, number, and status matches operational reality.
3. Converting inquiries into consultations, waitlist signups, and direct WhatsApp conversations.
4. Expanding what's possible and removing barriers through practical, grounded technology.

## Positioning

Grounded, honest, and high-craft local technology delivery. Unlike regional vendors who make vague corporate claims or foreign enterprises disconnected from local realities, SHERO operates with radical transparency:
- Clear, uninflated service offerings and direct communication.
- Strict honesty rules: verified battery health (≥ 90%) and Grade A++ standards for hardware, actual working hours (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00), zero fabricated metrics or logos.
- Proprietary venture products built specifically for local operating conditions (Merchander for social sellers, Pharmasyst for local pharmacies).

## Operating Context

- **Geographic Base:** Tamale, Ghana, serving Northern Ghana and nationwide.
- **Communication & Delivery Channels:** Direct WhatsApp (`wa.me` click-to-chat links) as the primary conversation bridge; local bus/station parcel dispatch for national fulfillment; on-site/remote IT and software support.
- **Operator Structure:** Faceless, single-operator model. No fabricated team photos, executive avatars, or "we are a team of global experts" phrasing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Offerings:**
  - *Custom Software:* Purpose-built web and business applications.
  - *Managed IT:* Setup, network configuration, and ongoing operational support.
  - *System & API Integrations:* Connecting business tools, payment gateways, and backend services.
  - *Hardware Shop:* Refurbished laptops and accessories (Note: development is intentionally paused and turned off on the live site for now, though retained in structural architecture).
  - *Venture Products (In Development):* Merchander (social commerce) and Pharmasyst (pharmacy management) collecting waitlist registrations. Healins (in validation, hidden from public site).
- **Operational Constraints:**
  - Support hours: Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 (never 24/7).
  - Client project proof restricted strictly to authorized production work: TrustCircle (for Samakose), Tastea, and Dajrim.
  - WhatsApp Phase 1 uses click-to-chat deep links via WhatsApp Business app (no automated bot billing overhead).

## Product Principles

1. **Truth Over Hype:** Never state a metric, SLA, integration, or status that cannot be verified in operational practice on launch day.
2. **People Over Technology:** Speak in plain human outcomes; explain what technology makes possible without hiding behind technical jargon.
3. **Radical Clarity:** Keep interfaces, specs, and workflows clean, quiet, and unambiguous—like a finely calibrated instrument.
4. **Earned Trust:** Build credibility incrementally through proven delivery, accurate facts, and dependable execution.
