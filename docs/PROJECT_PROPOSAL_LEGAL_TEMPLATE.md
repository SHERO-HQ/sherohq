# 2. Standard Master Service Agreement (Legal Terms)

### 1. Independent Contractor Relationship
SHERO acts solely as an independent technology service provider. No employment, agency, or partnership relationship is created.

### 2. Intellectual Property (IP) Ownership
* **Client IP**: Upon receipt of **100% full and final payment**, all custom source code, assets, and deliverables specifically created for `[Client Name]` become the exclusive property of the Client.
* **Studio Pre-Existing IP**: SHERO retains ownership of its pre-existing proprietary frameworks, libraries, design tokens, and modular tooling used to deliver the solution. The Client is granted a perpetual, royalty-free license to use these embedded components within the project.

### 3. Change Requests & Scope Adjustments
Any requested modifications outside Section 2 will be documented in a written **Change Order**, specifying the additional cost and impact on timeline. Work on revisions will commence only upon mutual written approval.

### 4. 30-Day Post-Launch Warranty
SHERO provides a **30-day technical warranty** following production deployment. During this window, any functional defects, errors, or bugs directly related to the contracted scope will be remediated at zero additional cost. The warranty excludes issues caused by third-party API outages or modifications made by unauthorized third parties.

### 5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
Both parties agree to hold all proprietary data, business processes, credentials, and technical specifications in strict confidence, and not disclose them to any unauthorized third party.

### 6. Payment Terms & Late Settlements
Invoices are payable within **7 business days** of issuance. Overdue balances may incur a 2% monthly late fee. SHERO reserves the right to suspend development or staging environments if milestone payments exceed 14 days past due.

### 7. Limitation of Liability
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. SHERO's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client under this contract.
