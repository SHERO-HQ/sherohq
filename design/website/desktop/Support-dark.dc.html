buying
Are your laptops new?−

No. They're UK-used, Grade A++, tested and cleaned before listing. Each listing shows the battery health.

What does the warranty cover?+

One week from delivery. If something we tested stops working, we'll repair or replace it.

How can I pay?+

MoMo, card (Visa or Mastercard), or cash on delivery.

delivery
Do you deliver outside Tamale?−

Yes, nationwide. Orders placed before 5:00 PM leave the same day, and delivery usually takes 12–72 hours from dispatch. Free over GHS 2,000.

Use your order number and phone number on the Track Order page. You don't need an account.
