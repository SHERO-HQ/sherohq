# WaitlistForm

Short interest form for product pages, with inline validation.

- Three or four fields at most: name, phone, business, one product-specific question.
- Labels above fields in `label` style; placeholders are real examples, never the label repeated.
- Errors in `danger` under the field, saying what to do: "Enter a 10-digit number, like 0244123456."
- Phone before email: most customers reply on WhatsApp.
